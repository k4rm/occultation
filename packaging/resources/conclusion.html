Installed

Rescan plug-ins in your host (Ableton Live: Preferences → Plug-Ins → Rescan; Logic rescans on launch) and look for Occultation under the Apokrypsi manufacturer.

The first time it opens a camera source, macOS will ask for camera permission — grant it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera for the host application, not for the plugin.

To uninstall, delete Occultation.vst3, Occultation.component and Occultation.app from the folders listed on the previous screens.
